BioCAT optics upgrade and downtime
###############################################################################

:category: users
:slug: optics-upgrade

BioCAT is approaching the end of a nearly 3 year long, 2 million dollar optics
upgrade project. This project was officially started in 2024 (though planning
started earlier than that) when BioCAT received $2 million in supplementary
funds from NIGMS to upgrade our x-ray optics to take full advantage of the new
APS-U source and provide enhanced capabilities for users. Once completed, this
will provide the beamline with smaller, more intense x-ray beams and improved
beam stability. This will improve operations and data quality for all users,
and let us offer significantly enhanced capabilities for microbeam work at BioCAT.

BioCAT will be operational as usual during the current 2026-3 run that started
in September. We will start installing the optics in January 2027. During the
installation and commissioning of these optics the beamline will not be
available for users. We are doing our utmost to ensure a smooth completion of
the project, but some downtime is unavoidable. We are currently planning on
having no user beamtime in the 2027-1 run, roughly February-April of 2027.
We plan to resume regular user operations in the 2027-2 run, which will start
around June 2027.


BioCAT user program during the downtime
=========================================

SAXS experiments
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

During the downtime, users are encouraged to make SAXS measurements at other bioSAXS
beamlines in the US. Users can directly apply for beamtime with these
facilities at any time, and BioCAT staff are happy to help with locating an
appropriate facility, planning experiments and data analysis as needed.

`Learn more about SAXS experiments at other facilities <{filename}/pages/users_optics_upgrade_saxs.rst>`_

Fiber diffraction experiments
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Muscle diffraction experiments are now routinely run at the LiX beamline at NSLS II.
During the APS-U dark period we also demonstrated that these experiments could be
successfully run at BL4-2 at SSRL and at ID7A at MacCHESS. Users can directly
apply for beamtime with these facilities at any time, and BioCAT staff are happy
to help with locating an appropriate facility, planning experiments and data
analysis as needed.

`Learn more about fiber diffraction experiments at other facilities <{filename}/pages/users_optics_upgrade_fiber.rst>`_
