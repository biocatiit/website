The Biophysics Collaborative Access Team (BioCAT) at Illinois Tech operates
the world-class biological small angle X-ray scattering and diffraction beamline
18ID at the Advanced Photon Source at Argonne National Laboratory (near Chicago,
IL). The facility is used by scientists and students from university, government,
and industrial labs around the world. These groups use BioCAT to conduct research
in areas from basic biomedical research to translational medicine.

BioCAT is seeking a Coordinator to support the operations of the facility. You
will assist with budget oversight and purchasing, including but not
limited to interfacing with both Illinois Tech and Argonne to make sure
orders are processed and delivered smoothly, working with vendors to procure
quotes and provide payment, verifying payroll, and tracking and reporting on
actual and projected spending to the facility director. You will also maintain
and update a record of all users of the BioCAT facility and their activities
and help prepare reports for both internal and external use. The position
involves other infrequent activities such as helping with hiring processes,
assisting with administration of workshops and meetings, preparing communications
for the user community, and other similar duties.The position is part time,
benefits eligible, and can be either hybrid or fully in person. Hours are flexible.
