BioCAT upgrade and downtime in early 2027
######################################################################################################

:date: 2026-08-17
:category: news
:slug: biocat-upgrade
:authors: BioCAT


.. image:: {static}/images/facility_thumbnail.jpg
    :class: img-rounded

BioCAT is approaching the end of a nearly 3 year long, 2 million dollar optics
upgrade project. This project was officially started in 2024 (though planning
started earlier than that) when BioCAT received $2 million in supplementary
funds from NIGMS to upgrade our x-ray optics to take full advantage of the
new APS-U source and provide enhanced capabilities for users. We are now in
the process of taking receipt of the new components, and have just received
the final approval needed from the APS to proceed with the installation. Once
completed, this will provide the beamline with smaller, more intense x-ray
beams and improved beam stability. This will improve operations and data
quality for all users, and let us offer significantly enhanced capabilities
for microbeam work at BioCAT.

BioCAT will be operational as usual during the upcoming 2026-3 run that starts
in September. We will start installing the optics in January 2027. During the
installation and commissioning of these optics the beamline will not be
available for users. We are doing our utmost to ensure a smooth completion
of the project, but some downtime is unavoidable. We are currently planning
on having no user beamtime in the 2027-1 run, roughly February-April of 2027.
We plan to resume regular user operations in the 2027-2 run, which will start
around June 2027.

BioCAT does still have beamtime available in the upcoming 2026-3 run, which
ends at the end of December, so if you do want beamtime at BioCAT between now
and next June we encourage you to contact a beamline scientist and start the
process of requesting time for this run now.

This upgrade will usher in a new era of science at BioCAT, as we replace our
30 year old x-ray optics with ones that we hope will sustain the facility for
the next 30 years of world class science. We look forward to continuing to
collaborate with and serve you, our users, and are excited for the cutting
edge new science this upgrade will make possible.
